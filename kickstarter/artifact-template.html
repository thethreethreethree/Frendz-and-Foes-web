What the reviewer actually said

“Projects focused on raising funds for general business expenses are not allowed… you mention that ‘Zwaptz is running now’, and explain that the funding would go toward ‘parts that cost money continuously — the valuation models, hosting, and reaching enough people.’ This currently frames the campaign as support for your ongoing operational costs… Instead, reframe the project so it’s clearly funding a specific, new, finite piece of work or deliverable.”

Kickstarter review · Zwaptz rejection

RULE 01

Never say it already works

PlayZoo is in development. The games sit behind GAMES_OPEN until the campaign closes, so “play it right now” was false as well as non-compliant.

RULE 02

Never fund a continuous cost

Hosting, servers, AI calls, general user acquisition. They may appear only in a sentence that denies your pledge pays for them.

RULE 03

Silence is not compliance

Deleting the claims was half the job. They asked for the opposite claim to be made — so the stage is now stated outright, twice.

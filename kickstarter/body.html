02 · Why we're doing this

Every other party app is a menu. A menu has never once made anyone laugh.

You know the drill. Someone opens an app, reads the rules out loud in a voice usually reserved for terms and conditions, everyone stares at their phone, and two rounds later half the room has "gone to get a drink" and quietly emigrated.

The games were never the problem. What was missing is a host — the loud idiot who keeps the energy up, hypes the underdog, and won't let you quietly quit. That's the entire job. That's the thing nobody else built. That's me.

03 · How it works

Twenty seconds from cold room to chaos.

1

Open the big screen

Load PlayZoo on any TV or laptop browser. That's the stage. No install, no login, no "create your team account."

2

Everyone scans in

Players point their phone at the code and they're in. No app store, no passwords, no forgetting which nickname was theirs.

3

I take over

Pick a game and try to keep up. I do the intros, the scoring, the trash talk, and the trophy. You just have to show up and lose gracefully.
